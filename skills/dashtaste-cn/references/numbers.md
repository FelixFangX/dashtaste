# Numbers and copy

Use 万/亿 compact units without a space: 1.23万, 4.56亿. Percentages have no space before %: 7.2%. Dates use explicit YYYY-MM-DD calendar fields.

Default deltaColors is red-up: up #D93A3A, down #167D4F. A page may switch to green-up by swapping these colours. Never mix conventions on one page. Always pair direction colour with ▲/▼; zero is flat and uses muted with no arrow. Missing or invalid comparison is –.

KPI cards show a value and two comparison rows: 同比 ▲ 7.2% and 环比 ▼ 1.3%. TOP N rankings use plain numerals, never medal emoji.

Date shortcuts: 今天、昨天、近7天、近30天、本月。

Use full-width punctuation in Chinese copy. Put a space between Hanzi and Latin/digits in prose: 本月收入 12 万元，较 Q1 增长。 Compact numbers remain 1.23万 and percentages remain 7.2%. Use short nouns and metric names; no explanatory captions.
