## Typography and spacing

Body: 14px. Minimum: 12px. KPI value: 24–32px. Heading: 18–24px. Use weight 400 for body and 600–700 for headings. Spacing: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32px. Keep alignment and card padding consistent.

Use system-ui, sans-serif. Use paper as the page and card surface.

All supplied categorical colours pass pairwise OKLab distance ≥ 0.08 under Machado 2009 severity 1.0 protan, deutan, and tritan simulations. No palette adjustments were needed. Body text pairs pass WCAG 4.5:1 in both modes.
